Privacy

The calculator runs in your browser. Salary figures you type are not sent to a CostToHire IL server. A downloaded briefing is a file created on your device.

Paid checkout is processed by Paddle as merchant of record. Paddle collects the billing details needed to take payment and issue a receipt. This site does not store card numbers.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub and Cloudflare (DNS) may process connection logs as described in their own notices.

Contact for privacy questions about this site: use the Paddle receipt email for payment data, or the seller contact that will be added after tax registration.
