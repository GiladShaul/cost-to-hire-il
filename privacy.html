Privacy

The v1 calculator runs in your browser. Salary inputs are not sent to a CostToHire IL server. A downloaded briefing is a file you create locally.

Paid checkout is processed by Paddle as merchant of record, under Paddle’s privacy notice. This site does not store card numbers. After a successful payment the briefing file is generated in your browser.

Seller contact details will be added after the owner supplies the public-facing wording. Do not email government identifiers to the repository.
