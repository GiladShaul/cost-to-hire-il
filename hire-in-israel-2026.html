What sits on top of gross

For a salaried employee living in Israel the employer typically pays national insurance on two bands (reduced up to ₪7,703, full up to ₪51,910), at least 6.5% pension tagmulim, and either the 6% expansion-order severance minimum or an 8.33% Section 14 reserve. Travel and convalescence are planning lines, not always a monthly payroll statute.
