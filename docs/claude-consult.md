# Claude consult — 2026-08-13

Consulted with `claude -p` on the model choice. No secrets were sent or returned.

**Question:** Pick one Israel-operable standalone online business under ILS 1,000 capital, minimal owner involvement, ILS 5,000 net / month in 3–6 months, and a path to ILS 100,000 / month. Compare employer-cost briefing, import landed-cost, accessibility generator, and a marketplace audit.

**Recommendation:** Employer-cost briefing → SMB SaaS.

**Why Claude rejected the others**

- Import / car landed cost: high error surface (green tax, HS codes), one-time consumer LTV, closest to customs-broker positioning.
- Accessibility generator: the money is certification, which is forbidden; remainder is a commodity.
- Marketplace audit: the owner has to sell and deliver. That is a job, not an asset.

**Economics Claude used (kept, then independently re-checked in DESIGN.md)**

- ILS 149 briefing, ILS 99 / month Pro.
- Month 4–6 mix of 25 briefings + 30 Pro ≈ ILS 5,900 net after ~8% MoR fees.
- Scale: firm/API ILS 1,500, seats, then other-country rate packs.
- Brand suggestion: CostToHire / EmployerCost. Public name used: **CostToHire IL**.

The chosen design follows that recommendation and the legal rules Claude listed (informational only, cite sources, MoR checkout, no state emblems, no client funds).
